# ToteKeeper visual source

Retrieved the public [ToteKeeper homepage](http://s195880366.onlinehome.us/index.html) on October 18, 2026. The browser text fetcher could not open it, but a direct HTTP request succeeded. `docs/reference/homepage.html` and `homepage.css` retain the HTML entry point and design source. The bundle was inspected to locate its shared logo geometry and imagery, then discarded to avoid shipping an unused frontend application.

`static/tokens.css` extracts DM Sans and DM Serif Display, cream `#f5f1e8`, navy `#244681`, dark navy `#192f59`, pale blue `#e0e8f8`, accent blue `#3e63b8`, ink `#243652`, muted text `#5f6a83`, thin warm borders and off-white panels. The portal shares the source's tote/lock mark, modest rectangular buttons, serif headings, compact cards and sidebar layout, with larger form text and touch targets for accessibility. Google font loading has local sans/serif fallbacks.

The public apartment/tote illustration is decorative dashboard art, never represented as a customer's uploaded photo. Customer photos are separate, authorized database objects. No homepage sample customer, price, capacity, contact information, delivery promise or commercial policy was activated as production data.
