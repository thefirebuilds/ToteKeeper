Empty handling is part of that cycle, not unlimited visits. Initial empty-tote delivery is not repeated automatically each year. The next service year provides the next included packed collection-and-return allowance and associated empty handling.

A partial return uses that package’s packed-return visit. Unused included visits expire at the service-year anniversary and do not roll over or convert to cash. Monthly billing does not refresh visits every month. A final return under Section 8 is separate from the ordinary allowance.

An additional packed return-and-recollection round trip costs $50 total for both legs at one approved service address. It is not $50 for each leg. The scope and price of other requested services must be accepted before booking or charging. Size swaps during an already scheduled visit carry no swap fee if the resulting mix remains within your allowance.
